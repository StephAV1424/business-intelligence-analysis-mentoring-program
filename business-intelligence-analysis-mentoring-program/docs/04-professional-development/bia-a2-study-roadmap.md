# Stephanie's Road to BIA A2

## A 3-Month, Mentor-Backed Study Plan

Built from Pedro's technical interview feedback, the BI Analysts Skill Matrix (A2 level), and the assigned EPAM mentor-led course.

---

# Where You Stand Today

Pedro's feedback was clear on one point that matters most:

> You are not starting from zero.

You already:

- Write intermediate SQL using CTEs and Window Functions
- Build Silver-to-Gold pipelines in Databricks
- Work confidently in Tableau
- Conduct requirements-gathering sessions
- Operate effectively within Scrum teams

What is missing is the theory and vocabulary required for A2-level business analysis judgment and decision-making.

This study plan focuses on closing five key gaps:

1. Data Warehousing Fundamentals
   - OLTP vs OLAP
   - Star Schema
   - Snowflake Schema
   - Slowly Changing Dimensions (SCD)

2. Data Modeling
   - Conceptual Models
   - Logical Models
   - Physical Models

3. Data Quality & Validation
   - Business Rules
   - Data Quality Frameworks

4. Business Analysis Methodologies & Artifacts
   - Requirements Lifecycle
   - Elicitation Techniques
   - MVPs
   - Wireframes
   - Prototypes

5. Risk & Project Management
   - Risk Identification
   - Prioritization Frameworks
   - Estimation Techniques

# How This Plan Works

|-----------|--------|

- A document
