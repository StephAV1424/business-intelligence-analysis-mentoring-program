# Sales Dashboard Discovery Plan

## Project Overview

The purpose of this discovery effort is to understand reporting challenges, identify reporting requirements, and define the foundation for a Sales Performance Dashboard.

---

# Business Problem

Regional sales teams currently rely on manual reporting processes that:

- Consume significant time.
- Limit visibility into sales performance.
- Delay decision-making.
- Create inconsistencies across regions.

---

# Project Goal

Design a Sales Dashboard that provides centralized visibility into sales performance metrics and supports data-driven decision-making.

# Discovery Objectives

1. Understand business goals.
2. Identify reporting pain points.
3. Define required KPIs.
4. Identify available data sources.
5. Determine dashboard success criteria.

---

# Discovery Activities

## Stakeholder Interviews

Purpose:

Gather business needs and expectations.

Deliverables:

- Interview notes
- Key findings
- Business goals

---

## Current State Analysis

Purpose:

Review current reporting process.

Deliverables:

- Existing reports inventory
- Process observations
- Gap analysis

---

## KPI Identification

Examples:

- Revenue
- Sales by Region
- Sales by Representative
- Quota Achievement
- Monthly Growth

---

## Data Source Assessment

Review:

- CRM Systems
- Sales Databases
- Existing Reports
- Operational Data Sources

# Success Criteria
