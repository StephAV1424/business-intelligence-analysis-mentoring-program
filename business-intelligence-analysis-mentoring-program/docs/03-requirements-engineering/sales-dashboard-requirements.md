# Sales Dashboard Requirements

## Project Vision

Provide Regional Sales Managers with a centralized, interactive dashboard that improves visibility into sales performance and reduces manual reporting efforts.

---

# User Persona

## James Rodriguez

### Role

Regional Sales Manager

### Experience

15+ Years

### Goals

- Monitor sales performance.
- Identify trends quickly.
- Improve decision making.
- Reduce reporting effort.

### Pain Points

- Manual reporting.
- Delayed visibility.
- Inconsistent metrics.
- Fragmented data sources.

---

# Business Requirements

## BR-001

The solution shall provide centralized KPI visibility.

---

## BR-002

The solution shall support regional performance analysis.

---

## BR-003

The solution shall reduce manual report creation.

---

## BR-004

The solution shall support automated reporting.

---

# Functional Requirements

## FR-001

Users shall filter dashboard data by region.

---

## FR-002

Users shall filter dashboard data by date range.

---

## FR-003

Users shall compare sales representatives.

---

## FR-004

Users shall visualize KPI trends.

---

## FR-005

Users shall export dashboard reports to PDF.

# Epics

## Epic 1

Sales Performance Monitoring

### User Story 1

As a Regional Sales Manager,
I want to filter data by region,
so that I can identify underperforming areas.

### User Story 2

As a Regional Sales Manager,
I want to monitor KPI trends,
so that I can proactively respond to performance issues.

## Epic 2

Reporting Automation

### User Story 3

As a Regional Sales Manager,
I want automated report delivery,
so that I spend less time developing reports manually.

so that I can share information with leadership.

# Non-Functional Requirements

## Performance
